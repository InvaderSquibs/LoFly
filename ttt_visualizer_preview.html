Fly Brain Console

About this experiment & what to trust
What's real: the connectivity graph, neuron types, and neurotransmitter signs are the actual published MaleCNS v1.0 annotations, streamed and filtered down to a 6,205-neuron induced subgraph covering the olfactory→mushroom-body→descending pathway. Every simulation is a genuine leaky-integrate-and-fire run over that real wiring (Brian2, parameters from Shiu et al. 2024) — nothing here is a lookup table.
What's a stand-in: a board cell maps to one ORN glomerulus and a move maps to one descending neuron by connectivity-verified association, not because the fly brain evolved to play tic-tac-toe. The connectome weights are frozen throughout — the only thing that "learns" is a 9-value bias added on top (reservoir-computing style), which is a deliberately simple first pass, not a claim that the brain itself is adapting.
Honest caveat: the move-preference pattern the network produces looks similar in relative shape across different board states (mostly scaled by how much of the board is filled), so a fair amount of the win-rate gain above comes from the learned bias finding good default cells rather than the connectome sharply discriminating between board layouts. A board-state-aware policy is the natural next step.
Bias values, once nudged for a while, run into a fixed ±60 Hz clip — visible in the learning curve as the rate of improvement flattening. That's the ceiling of this simple global-bias approach, not the ceiling of the connectome.
